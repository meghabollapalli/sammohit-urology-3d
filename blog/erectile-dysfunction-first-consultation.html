Erectile dysfunction: what the first consultation involves

22 November 2026Draft · awaiting the doctor’s review

Erectile dysfunction means difficulty getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex. It is common and it is a medical condition with causes that can be looked for. Many men delay seeing a doctor because they are unsure what the visit involves. This article describes it.

The conversation

The consultation is one to one and private. The doctor asks how long the problem has been present, whether it happens every time, and about your general health, medicines, sleep, smoking and alcohol. Conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure and low testosterone are common causes, and so is stress.

Examination and tests

An examination is done only if it is needed and with your consent. Typical tests are:

  • Blood sugar and cholesterol
  • Hormone tests, including testosterone
  • Other tests if the first results suggest them

Treatment options

Treatment depends on the cause. The doctor may discuss:

  1. Treating the underlying condition, such as diabetes or a hormone problem
  2. Tablets, when they are suitable for you
  3. Penile injection therapy (Bimix or Trimix), when tablets are not enough or cannot be used. The dose is set in the clinic and you are taught how to use it safely.

Do not buy medicines for erectile dysfunction without a prescription. Some are unsafe with heart medicines.

Premature ejaculation

Premature ejaculation is a separate condition and is also treatable, with medicine and practical techniques. You can raise both concerns at the same visit.

This article gives general information and does not replace a consultation.
